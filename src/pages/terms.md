## 1. What the app does

PasswordEpic stores credentials you enter, encrypted on your Android device, and
can fill them into other apps and websites through Android's Autofill framework.

## 2. Your passcode, and what we cannot do

:::danger We cannot recover your passcode or your vault

The app is designed so that neither we nor anyone else can decrypt your data
without your passcode and your device. That is the point of it — and it means
there is no reset link, no recovery question, and no support override.

**If you forget your passcode, or lose the device, the data is permanently
gone.** Backups and exports are tied to the device that created them and will
not open elsewhere.

:::

You are responsible for remembering your passcode and for keeping your own
copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.

## 3. One device per account

On the paid tiers, an account is active on one device at a time. To move to
another device you must reset the account from the device that currently holds
it, which erases its data. If you no longer have that device, contact support —
we can release the account, but **this does not recover the vault**.
